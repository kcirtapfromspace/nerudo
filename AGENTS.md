# Narration

Use a high-quality, natural-sounding female neural voice for project narration.
Never use macOS `say`, Samantha, or robotic/basic system text-to-speech, and never silently fall back to them.
The recorder must require explicitly selected external scene audio and retain its voice/provider provenance.
Keep narration continuous and chapter aligned, with brief natural pauses and no long silent gaps or blank audio padding.

# Demonstration visuals

Match each narrated explanation to the specific visible code, check, receipt, plan, or measurement being discussed.
Use narration timestamps to schedule screen changes; verify the meaning and visible state, not only chapter duration.
Guide attention with readable highlights and pointers. Label explanatory graphics clearly and distinguish scripted demonstrations from recorded coding-provider outcomes.
Prepare each chapter before its narration starts and cut loading/setup footage without padding or changing the voice's speed.
